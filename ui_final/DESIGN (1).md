## Brand & Style

This design system targets grassroots football club managers, amateur league organizers, and local players who demand the clarity, authority, and tactical polish of premier sports platforms (FotMob, Premier League, Sofascore). The visual character balances clean athletic professionalism with utilitarian clarity.

Key tonal anchors:
- **Athletic Authority:** Grounded, high-legibility sports journalism feel. It favors high information density delivered with structured whitespace rather than decorative clutter.
- **Matchday Tactical Clarity:** Immediate visibility for scores, fixture times, player ratings, card penalties, and league tables under bright outdoor daylight or fast sideline checks.
- **Crafted Modernism:** Subtle tonal layers, crisp slate separation borders, authentic emerald pitch grass accents, and refined metals (Gold, Silver, Bronze) for standings. Neon gradients, sci-fi cyber glows, and skeuomorphic field textures are strictly avoided.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the crisp, natural atmosphere of an immaculate morning matchday pitch paired with modern slate editorial typography.

- **Primary (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Pitch Emerald. Reserved for key match actions, live status indicators, confirmed team selections, and active navigational tabs. Never overused on giant background floods; it acts as a decisive physical beacon.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Stadium Navy Slate. Anchors high-priority headers, score digits, team acronyms, and high-contrast table headers.
- **Tertiary & Accents:**
  - Ruby / Live Match Red (`#DC2626`): Red cards, live minute pulse badges, injury alerts.
  - Amber / Warning Gold (`#D97706`): Yellow cards, pending player verifications, second-tier podium indicators.
  - Pitch Silver (`#64748B`) & Bronze (`#B45309`): League table rank distinctions and historic performance metrics.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Background Base: `#F8FAFC` (Cool Slate Canvas).
  - Surface Card: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Studio White).
  - Subtle Borders & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` (Structural Grid Line).
  - Muted Text / Sublabels: `#64748B` (Secondary Stat Slate).

## Typography

Typography pairs **Chivo** for athletic impact, scorelines, team codes, and headers with **Inter** for data tables, statistics, form guides, and player profiles.

- **Chivo (Headlines & Data Labels):** Delivers a modern, structured athletic broadcast presence. Used with uppercase tracking for league acronyms, minute counters, table column headers (`PTS`, `GD`, `MP`), and live match timers.
- **Inter (Body & Administrative Data):** Offers neutral, legibility-focused reading for tactical lineups, squad rosters, player stats, and admin inputs.
- Numerical scores and clock times always use tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shift during live match updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system anchored by modular sports cards and table containers.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Fixture cards stack vertically; tables scroll horizontally with frozen team name columns.
  - Tablet (`768px – 1024px`): 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Roster tables and pitch line-ups split 50/50.
  - Desktop (`> 1024px`): 12 columns, max-width `1280px`, `margin: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Enables 3-column splits (League Table [4 col] | Live Scorecard / Pitch Visualizer [5 col] | Match Feed & Form [3 col]).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a 4px-based baseline cadence. Dense statistical cards utilize `space-sm` and `space-md`, while editorial overview dashboards expand with `space-xl` and `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via tonal layering and structural borders (`#E2E8F0`) rather than deep drop shadows or glow effects.

- **Ground Layer:** Base app viewport background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Tables, Rosters):** `#FFFFFF` with a single 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`) and an imperceptible diffused ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Raised Interactive Layer (Dropdown menus, Match Center Drawers, Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` bordered by `#CBD5E1` with elevation shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Active Selection & Focus States:** Outlines use an interior or offset 2px ring in Pitch Emerald (`#059669`) with zero blur or outer illumination.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, establishing a precise, athletic feel that maximizes usable surface area for stats and data grids.

- **Default UI Elements (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Stat chips, data table containers, text inputs, segmented toggle switches, and team crest badges.
- **Cards & Match Banners (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Match scorecards, roster drawers, and summary panels.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live match time badges (`LIVE 74'`), form guide badges (`W`, `D`, `L`), and player jersey number pins on tactical pitch boards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Pitch Emerald (`#059669`), text `#FFFFFF`, font Chivo 13px semi-bold (`label-lg`). Hover: `#047857`. Active: `#065F46`.
- **Secondary:** Stadium Slate (`#0F172A`), text `#FFFFFF`. Used for administrative actions like "Export Lineup" or "Publish Match".
- **Outline / Neutral:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Soft ruby background `#FEF2F2`, 1px border `#FCA5A5`, text `#DC2626`.

### Chips & Form Guides
- **Match Form Pills:** Compact 20x20px circles or rounded pills. 
  - Win: `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, label "W".
  - Draw: `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, label "D".
  - Loss: `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, label "L".
- **Podium Rank Chips (Standings):** 
  - 1st (Gold): `#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text.
  - 2nd (Silver): `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text.
  - 3rd (Bronze): `#FFEDD5` background, `#9A3412` text.
  - Relegation Zone: `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text.

### Scorecards & Match Rows
- High-contrast two-club layout. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `#E2E8F0`. 
- Club names in Chivo medium (`#0F172A`), scores centered in large tabular bold (`stat-score`, `#0F172A`).
- Subtitle provides kickoff time, pitch number (e.g., "Pitch 3 - District 7"), and referee assignment in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Data Tables (Standings & Player Stats)
- Header: `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase tracking `label-caps` (`#475569`), 36px row height, bottom border 1px `#E2E8F0`.
- Rows: 44px standard height, alternating hover `#F8FAFC`. Team name pinned left; stats columns (`P`, `W`, `D`, `L`, `GF`, `GA`, `GD`, `PTS`) right-aligned with tabular numerals.
- Clean horizontal separation with `#F1F5F9` dividers, no vertical grid lines.

### Admin In-Situ Toggles
- Compact iOS/clean SaaS style switches (36x20px). Track off: `#CBD5E1`. Track on: `#059669`. No shadow glows. Thumb is crisp `#FFFFFF` circle. Used for "Paid Match Fee", "Confirmed Attendance", "Starter / Sub".

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Padding: `0.5rem 0.75rem`. Focus: border `#059669`, ring 1px `#059669`.
